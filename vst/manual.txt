= START
# PotentialKits: a drum kit from your own samples
Reaches into your sample folders and pulls out 16 sounds for the pads: a kick, a snare, hats and so on, plus a few surprises. Lock what you like, roll the rest, then save it as a real MPC drum program.

- Load it as a plugin instrument (a plugin program). The first time, it scans your drives for samples.
- It looks in the Expansions and Samples folders on every drive. Pick another folder on the SETUP page.
- Tap NEW KIT, a KIT STYLE on the DIP page, or ROLL.
- Play the pads, or tap a tile on the KIT page to hear it.

The full PotentialSuite manual: github.com/PotentialBreakfast/potential-plugins

= KIT
# The KIT page
- The 16 tiles are laid out like the MPC's pads (A01 bottom left). A tile shows its sample; * means locked.
- Tap a tile to hear it and select it. With FOLLOW PLAYED PAD on (POOL page), playing a pad selects it too.
- LOCK keeps a pad's sample through NEW KIT, styles and ROLL. REROLL gives the pad a new one. CLEAR empties it.
- GAIN, PITCH (12 semitones each way) and DECAY (100 % is the whole sample) shape the pad.
- CHOKE: pads in the same group cut each other off, like closed and open hats. OFF plays freely.
- FAVOURITE makes a sample twice as likely to come up again. REJECT means it never comes up.

= DIP
# Styles, ROLL and USER kits
- KIT STYLES set what each pad draws from, its pitch, decay and choke, then dip: CLASSIC, BOOM BAP, LO-FI, TRAP, HOUSE, DUSTY JAZZ, PERCUSSION, ELECTRO and WEIRD. Locked pads are left alone.
- ROLL: MILD gives up to four pads a new sample. MEDIUM makes a new kit with a little pitch and decay. WILD mixes up what the pads draw from too.
- USER KITS: tap SAVE, then a slot, to keep the whole kit. Tap a slot to bring it back, in any project.
- NEW KIT, CLEAR ALL, UNLOCK ALL, MATCH LEVELS (evens out loud and quiet pads) and EXPORT KIT.

= EXPORT
# Saving the kit
- EXPORT KIT writes a normal MPC drum program (.xpm) with your 16 samples on pads A01 to A16. Load it from the browser like any kit.
- Pads with PITCH, DECAY or GAIN changed are saved as you hear them, as new WAV files. Other pads are copied as they are.
- CHOKE becomes the program's mute groups.
- With LINK TO ORIGINALS (SETUP page) the samples are linked, not copied: PITCH is set on the pad instead, and decay and gain are not saved.
- Kits go to PotentialKits/Kits on the plugin's drive, or the folder you pick on the SETUP page.
- Your project remembers the kit, the locks and every setting. USER KITS live in PotentialSuite Presets/PotentialKits.

= POOL
# What a pad draws from
- The POOL page shows the selected pad's sounds. A lit key is a sound the pad can draw from.
- Tap keys to add or remove sounds: make pad 1 draw kicks and bass, say. Then REROLL it on the KIT page.
- Sounds are sorted by their folder and file names: a folder called Kicks, or a file called punchy_kick_01.wav, is a kick.
- Files in folders with no clear name end up in OTHER, which only a few pads draw from.

= HELP
# Good to know
- Pads play MIDI notes 0 to 15 and 36 to 51, so the pads, a keyboard and the sequencer all work.
- SKIP LOOPS leaves out files that look like loops. MAX SIZE leaves out big files. Press RESCAN after changing either.
- Very long files are cut to 15 seconds on the pad: PotentialKits is for one-shots.
- If you move or delete a sample, its tile shows ! before the name.
- Looks: Print Plate, Night Rack or Colour Block, on the SETUP page.
- PotentialKits is a fork of Lucky Dip by sd88me. Thanks to him.

Ideas, bugs and criticism are all welcome: github.com/PotentialBreakfast/mpc-vst-potentialkits
