= START
# PotentialKits: a drum kit from your own samples
Reaches into your sample folders and pulls out 16 sounds for the pads: a kick, a snare, hats and so on, plus a few surprises. Lock what you like, roll the rest, then save it.

- Load it as a plugin instrument (a plugin program). The first time, it scans your drives for samples.
- It looks in the Expansions and Samples folders on every drive. Pick another folder on the SETUP page.
- Tap NEW KIT or ROLL on the KIT page, or a KIT STYLE on the STYLES page.
- Play the pads, or tap a tile on the KIT page to hear it.

The full PotentialSuite manual: github.com/PotentialBreakfast/potential-plugins

= KIT
# The KIT page
- The 16 tiles are laid out like the MPC's pads (A01 bottom left). A tile shows its sample; * means locked.
- Tap a tile to hear it and select it. Playing a pad selects it too (FOLLOW PLAYED PAD, SETUP page).
- TYPE: what the pad draws from. Tap the arrows: KICK, SNARE, CLAP ... BASS, SYNTH ... The pad gets a new sound of that type straight away. AUTO is the pad's usual sound.
- LOCK keeps a pad's sample through NEW KIT, styles and ROLL. REROLL gives the pad a new one.
- GAIN, PITCH (12 semitones each way) and DECAY (100 % is the whole sample) shape the pad.
- CHOKE: pads in the same group cut each other off, like closed and open hats. OFF plays freely.

= STYLES
# Styles and ROLL
- KIT STYLES set what each pad draws from, its pitch, decay and choke, then pick a kit: CLASSIC, BOOM BAP, LO-FI, TRAP, HOUSE, DUSTY JAZZ, PERCUSSION, ELECTRO and WEIRD.
- ROLL KIT (or ROLL on the KIT page): MILD gives up to four pads a new sample. MEDIUM makes a new kit with a little pitch and decay. WILD mixes up what the pads draw from too.
- Locked pads are always left alone. UNLOCK ALL frees them; CLEAR ALL empties every unlocked pad.

= SAVE
# Saving the kit
- USER KITS: tap SAVE (it says NOW TAP A SLOT), then a slot. Tap a filled slot to bring the kit back, in any project. They live in PotentialSuite Presets/PotentialKits beside your VST2 Plugins folder.
- EXPORT KIT writes a normal MPC drum program (.xpm) with your 16 samples on pads A01 to A16. Load it from the browser like any kit.
- Pads with PITCH, DECAY or GAIN changed are saved as you hear them, as new WAV files. CHOKE becomes the program's mute groups.
- MATCH LEVELS evens out loud and quiet pads first.
- Kits go to PotentialKits/Kits on the plugin's drive, or the folder you pick on the SETUP page. With LINK TO ORIGINALS the samples are linked, not copied.

= HELP
# Good to know
- Pads play MIDI notes 0 to 15 and 36 to 51, so the pads, a keyboard and the sequencer all work.
- Sounds are sorted by their folder and file names: a folder called Kicks, or a file called punchy_kick_01.wav, is a kick.
- SKIP LOOPS leaves out files that look like loops. MAX SIZE leaves out big files. Press RESCAN after changing either.
- Very long files are cut to 15 seconds on the pad. If you move or delete a sample, its tile shows ! before the name.
- Looks: Print Plate, Night Rack, Colour Block or Vintage, on the SETUP page.
- PotentialKits is a fork of Lucky Dip by sd88me. Thanks to him.
